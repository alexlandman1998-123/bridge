# Rentals CRM Phase 11 — FICA Readiness

Phase 11 adds a structured, lead-level FICA readiness checklist for tenant leads in `Screening pending` or `FICA pending`.

The four tracked items are identity, proof of address, bank details, and source of funds. Completing FICA requires every item to be marked `verified` and an evidence reference. The checklist is an internal readiness record; it is not a legal compliance opinion, a document-store replacement, or an application approval.
