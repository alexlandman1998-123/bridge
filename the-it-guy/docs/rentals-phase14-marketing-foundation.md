# Rentals Phase 14 — Media and internal marketing readiness

Rental media is stored in the private `rental-vacancy-media` bucket under an organisation and Vacancy-bound path. RLS allows only staff with access to the owning Rental property to upload, read or remove it.

Readiness is internal only: title, description, at least one media item and the right Vacancy lifecycle state are required. It has no portal-ready or external-publishing state.
