The commission calculation must identify any amount excluded from the purchase price. If there is no agreed exclusion, the purchase price is the price stated for the Property in the binding sale agreement. Only one total commission basis may be selected. The combined seller fee and any VAT attributable to each agency's portion must be calculable before signature. No agency may charge VAT it is not lawfully entitled to charge.

## 1 Joint appointment and mandate period

The Seller appoints Agency A and Agency B together as the two agencies authorised to market the Property during the recorded mandate period. Both agencies accept this same agreement. The Seller must not appoint a third agency during that period unless the Seller and both agencies expressly agree to vary the appointment.

The Seller may receive direct enquiries and sell privately. An independent private sale does not attract commission under this mandate if neither agency was its effective cause. Each agency acts within its own professional responsibilities; neither may bind the Seller or the other agency without separate express authority.

The joint appointment takes effect only after all required seller signers and both authorised agency signatories have signed, both agencies have received the completed and signed prescribed Mandatory Disclosure Form, and the required authority to sign has been established. Its effective start is the later of that joint acceptance and the recorded start date. Acceptance must not be backdated. An agency must not act under this joint appointment while the other required acceptance is outstanding.

The mandate period ends on the recorded end date or effective earlier cancellation. The Seller directs termination on the recorded end date. The agencies must give any legally required expiry notice and record the Seller's instructions about expiry or renewal. Mandatory continuation requirements, if applicable, remain effective; this direction does not waive consumer rights. The restriction to two agencies must not renew by silence. A further restricted appointment requires express agreement to a new defined period.

The Seller decides whether to accept, reject or counter any offer. The asking price is a marketing instruction, not a guaranteed sale price. This mandate does not authorise either agency to sign a sale agreement, accept an offer or bind the Seller to a sale.

## 2 Agency services and marketing expenses

Each agency undertakes to perform the tasks assigned to it in the completed joint marketing plan. The agencies must coordinate advertising, access, introductions and offers to avoid conflicting instructions. They must advertise at the agreed asking price, arrange viewings at times agreed with the Seller and present written offers promptly. The nominated reporting agency must give the Seller a written update at least weekly while actively marketing, including the activity and feedback supplied by both agencies. Nomination does not remove either agency's own duties.

## 3 Seller authority and mandatory disclosure

Each seller signer signs as an owner or in the recorded capacity for the legal owner. Before accepting the mandate, each agency must establish the necessary co-owner, spouse, company, close corporation, trust or representative authority and consent. Being the main contact does not authorise a person to sign for another owner. The applicable evidence and required signers must be identified in the authority record.

Neither agency may accept this mandate before receiving the Seller's fully completed and signed prescribed Mandatory Disclosure Form. Each must ensure that a prospective purchaser intending to make an offer receives that form and that the form is dealt with as required in the resulting sale agreement. Sharing a form reference does not replace confirming access to the actual form. This mandate does not replace or amend the prescribed form.

The Seller must disclose known material facts about the Property and promptly report material changes to both agencies. Each agency must disclose material facts within its knowledge and must not describe the Property contrary to the information available to it. Each agency and practitioner must hold the certificates and registrations required for the work performed. Recording an FFC number does not itself establish compliance or a right to commission.

## 4 One total commission and agency allocation

Subject to lawful entitlement and the recorded exclusions, the one total commission is earned if an appointed agency was the effective cause of a binding sale concluded during the effective joint mandate period and every suspensive condition is fulfilled or lawfully waived. After the joint appointment ends, only the introduced-buyer clause below can establish an entitlement under this mandate, subject to rights that cannot lawfully be limited.

Effective cause means that an agency's work brought about the sale, assessed on all the circumstances. An introduction or a name on a buyer list is not sufficient on its own. The Seller is not obliged to accept an offer or pay commission merely because an agency produced an interested buyer.

Both agencies agree that the Seller's total commission liability under this mandate cannot exceed the recorded combined fee, including VAT as agreed and lawfully chargeable. The agency that establishes entitlement receives the commission unless an express allocation annexure, accepted by the Seller and both agencies, provides for sharing. There is no automatic equal split and no second full commission under this agreement.

Both agencies must disclose competing introductions and cooperate to record the allocation before the Seller concludes a sale. If entitlement remains disputed, the agencies must first seek a recorded resolution and may use lawful dispute processes. Their allocation dispute does not increase the Seller's total fee under this mandate or require the Seller to guarantee payment of two commissions. Any retention of a disputed amount requires a lawful arrangement with the person holding the funds or an appropriate legal order; this clause alone does not authorise a conveyancer to withhold funds.

Commission is payable on registration of transfer from the proceeds, against valid invoices showing the agreed allocation and each agency's lawful VAT treatment, with lawful payment authority. Any change to that timing requires express recorded agreement consistent with applicable law. There is no automatic accelerated commission payment merely because a sale fails to transfer; a separate claim for loss must be lawfully established.

This shared fee limit binds only the parties to this mandate. Existing or separate agency agreements must be disclosed and addressed expressly; this agreement does not extinguish an outside agency's claim. Cancellation alone, an unsuccessful offer, or failure to find a purchaser does not automatically earn commission.

## 5 Introduced buyers after the mandate ends

If the agreed buyer protection period is zero, no post-mandate commission protection arises under this clause. Otherwise, each agency seeking protection must deliver its written protected-buyer list to the Seller and the other agency's recorded notice contacts within five business days after expiry or effective cancellation. A jointly delivered list must identify the agency responsible for each introduction. The list must identify the buyer, introduction date and relevant agency involvement without unnecessary identity documents.

Commission under this clause requires all of the following: the claiming agency introduced the listed buyer during the effective joint appointment; its list was delivered within that deadline; a binding sale agreement with that buyer was concluded within the agreed number of calendar days after the appointment ended; that agency was the effective cause of the sale; and all suspensive conditions were fulfilled or lawfully waived. A shared or duplicated buyer entry does not create a second seller fee.

The protection window runs from the day after the joint appointment ends. Delivery of a list does not restart or extend it. Fulfilment of suspensive conditions and transfer may occur later if the sale agreement was concluded within the window. The exclusions and one total fee and allocation rules still apply. A sale outside this clause does not create a commission entitlement under this mandate; rights that cannot lawfully be limited remain effective.

## 6 Cancellation and changes

The Seller may cancel the joint appointment before expiry by giving 20 business days' notice in writing or another recorded form to both agencies' listed notice contacts. This is a contractual right for this fixed-period mandate, including where statutory fixed-term cancellation rights do not apply. A more favourable mandatory cancellation or cooling-off right remains available. Both agencies may agree to an earlier effective cancellation date. Where a mandatory notice rule differs, that rule takes priority.

Each agency must acknowledge notice promptly and record the common effective date. Marketing must stop when the appointment ends, subject to any work required by law. A valid cancellation does not depend on agreement to a disputed charge or signature of a replacement document.

Any cancellation charge must be lawful, reasonable and supported by an itemised explanation. Both agencies must present a coordinated account that identifies their own work and commitments and prevents duplicate recovery. Full commission, the remaining term's estimated commission or a fixed percentage of the asking price is not automatically due because the Seller cancels. Lawfully owing approved expenses and commission already earned retain their payment terms. Refunds or credits must be given where required.

An agency seeking to cancel for a material seller breach must notify the Seller and the other agency, identify the breach and allow 20 business days to remedy it before cancellation, unless law requires a different course. An agency must cease work it may no longer lawfully perform and notify the other parties promptly. If one agency's appointment ends, joint marketing must pause and the remaining parties must expressly agree a replacement appointment before continuing under different terms. The mandate must not silently convert into an exclusive appointment for the remaining agency.

Material changes to parties, authority, property, price, period, commission, allocation, exclusions or wording must be recorded and accepted by every affected contracting party. A sent or signed copy must not be edited in place. A material change creates a replacement version, identifies what it supersedes and obtains the necessary fresh signatures. Statutory cancellation and notices do not require unanimous agreement.

## 7 Information notices and records

Each agency may process information on a lawful basis for identity and authority checks, FICA compliance, marketing, administering this appointment and the resulting transaction. Each must provide its identified privacy notice, protect the information and retain records only as required or permitted by law. Sharing between agencies must be limited to what is reasonably necessary for this appointment and have a lawful basis. Access to the mandate does not itself grant access to the Seller's complete FICA or identity-document file. Optional promotional marketing requires a separate lawful choice; signing this mandate does not provide blanket promotional consent to either agency.

Notices must identify the mandate and be sent to the recorded contacts or another contact subsequently notified in a recorded form. The sender must retain evidence of delivery to each intended recipient. A valid notice is not defeated by an absent acknowledgement; receipt and timing remain subject to applicable law.

The completed terms, joint marketing schedule, allocation rule, exclusions, Special Conditions and identified annexures form this mandate. An agreed Special Condition must identify the provision it changes and cannot remove mandatory rights. No party guarantees a sale or a particular price. This appointment does not compel the Seller to select a particular attorney or other ancillary service provider.

The parties may sign on paper or through an electronic route approved as lawful for this mandate. Every required seller signer and both authorised agency signatories must review and sign the same identified version in their recorded capacity. Electronic signing of this mandate does not authorise electronic execution of a property sale agreement. The Seller and both agencies must receive a complete copy and any signing evidence.
