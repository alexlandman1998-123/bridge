## 1 Appointment and mandate period

The Seller appoints the Agency on a non-exclusive basis to market the Property and seek a purchaser. The Seller may appoint other agencies, receive enquiries directly and sell privately. Signing this mandate alone does not earn commission, and an independent private sale does not attract commission under this mandate if the Agency was not its effective cause.

The appointment takes effect only after all required parties have signed, the Agency has received the completed and signed prescribed Mandatory Disclosure Form, and the required authority to sign has been established. Its effective start is the later of that acceptance and the recorded start date. Acceptance must not be backdated.

Unless the parties expressly choose a fixed end date, the appointment continues until cancelled. If they choose a fixed end date, the Seller directs termination on that date and the Agency must follow any legally required expiry notice and continuation process. Silence does not create an exclusive appointment or a new fixed period.

The Seller decides whether to accept, reject or counter any offer. The asking price is a marketing instruction, not a guaranteed sale price. This mandate does not authorise the Agency to sign a sale agreement, accept an offer or bind the Seller to a sale.

## 2 Agency services and marketing expenses

The Agency undertakes to carry out the completed marketing plan, advertise at the agreed asking price, arrange viewings at times agreed with the Seller, present written offers promptly, and assist with negotiations. It must give the Seller a written update at least weekly while actively marketing, covering enquiries, viewings, feedback and offers. The parties must record agreed access arrangements and any occupied-property restrictions.

## 3 Seller authority and mandatory disclosure

Each seller signer signs as an owner or in the recorded capacity for the legal owner. Before accepting the mandate, the Agency must establish the necessary co-owner, spouse, company, close corporation, trust or representative authority and consent. Being the main contact does not authorise a person to sign for another owner. The applicable evidence and required signers must be identified in the authority record.

The Agency may accept this mandate only after receiving the Seller's fully completed and signed prescribed Mandatory Disclosure Form. It must give that form to a prospective purchaser intending to make an offer and ensure the form is dealt with as required in the resulting sale agreement. This mandate does not replace or amend the prescribed form.

The Seller must disclose known material facts about the Property and promptly report material changes. The Agency must disclose material facts within its knowledge and must not describe the Property contrary to the information available to it. The Agency must hold the certificates and registrations required for the work it performs. Recording an FFC number does not itself establish compliance or a right to commission.

## 4 Commission and competing introductions

Subject to the Agency's lawful entitlement and the recorded exclusions, commission is earned only if the Agency was the effective cause of a binding sale concluded during the effective appointment, and every suspensive condition is fulfilled or lawfully waived. After the appointment ends, only the introduced-buyer clause below can establish a commission entitlement under this mandate, subject to rights that cannot lawfully be limited.

Effective cause means that the Agency's work brought about the sale, assessed on all the circumstances. Introducing a buyer, arranging a viewing, holding this mandate or entering a name on a buyer list does not, on its own, establish entitlement. The Seller is not obliged to accept an offer or pay commission merely because the Agency produced an interested buyer.

Commission is calculated using the agreed basis and VAT treatment. It is payable on registration of transfer from the proceeds, against a valid invoice and with lawful payment authority. Any change to that payment timing requires an express recorded agreement consistent with applicable law. There is no automatic accelerated commission payment merely because a sale fails to transfer; a separate claim for loss must be lawfully established.

The Seller must disclose known competing introductions before accepting an offer, and the Agency must disclose a known competing claim promptly. The parties must address the claim expressly before concluding the sale. This mandate does not bind another agency, extinguish its agreement or guarantee that another agency has no claim. Cancellation alone, an unsuccessful offer, or failure to find a purchaser does not automatically earn commission.

## 5 Introduced buyers after the mandate ends

If the agreed buyer protection period is zero, no post-mandate commission protection arises under this clause. Otherwise, the Agency must deliver a written protected-buyer list to the Seller's recorded notice contact within five business days after expiry or effective cancellation. The list must identify each buyer and give the introduction date and a reasonable description of the Agency's relevant involvement, without unnecessary identity documents.

Commission under this clause requires all of the following: the Agency introduced the listed buyer during the effective appointment; the list was delivered within that deadline; a binding sale agreement with that buyer was concluded within the agreed number of calendar days after the appointment ended; the Agency was the effective cause of that sale; and all suspensive conditions were fulfilled or lawfully waived. A name on a list is not sufficient.

The protection window runs from the day after the appointment ends. Delivery of the buyer list does not restart or extend it. Fulfilment of suspensive conditions and transfer may occur later if the sale agreement was concluded within the window. The agreed exclusions still apply. A sale outside this clause does not create a commission entitlement under this mandate; rights that cannot lawfully be limited remain effective.

## 6 Cancellation and changes

The Seller may cancel this open appointment at any time by notice in writing or another recorded form through the Agency's listed notice contact. Cancellation takes effect when the notice is received, or on a later date expressly stated by the Seller. This immediate contractual cancellation right also applies if the parties chose a fixed end date.

The Agency must acknowledge notice promptly, record the effective date and stop marketing when the appointment ends, subject to any work required by law. A valid cancellation does not depend on the Agency agreeing to a disputed charge or signing a replacement document. The Agency may end an indefinite appointment on reasonable recorded notice, taking account of outstanding offers and any applicable legal requirement.

No cancellation penalty is payable under this open mandate. Commission already earned and separately approved expenses lawfully incurred remain subject to their agreed payment terms. Refunds or credits must be given where required. More favourable mandatory cancellation or cooling-off rights remain available.

Material changes to parties, authority, property, price, period, commission, exclusions or wording must be recorded and accepted by every affected contracting party. A sent or signed copy must not be edited in place. A material change creates a replacement version, identifies what it supersedes and obtains the necessary fresh signatures. Statutory cancellation and notices do not require unanimous agreement.

## 7 Information notices and records

The Agency may process information on a lawful basis for identity and authority checks, FICA compliance, marketing, administering this appointment and the resulting transaction. It must provide the identified privacy notice, restrict access to what is reasonably necessary, protect the information and retain records only as required or permitted by law. Optional promotional marketing requires a separate lawful choice; signing this mandate does not provide blanket promotional consent.

Notices must identify the mandate and be sent to the recorded contacts or another contact subsequently notified in a recorded form. The sender must retain evidence of delivery. A valid notice is not defeated by an absent acknowledgement; receipt and timing remain subject to applicable law.

The completed terms, marketing schedule, exclusions, Special Conditions and identified annexures form this mandate. An agreed Special Condition must identify the provision it changes and cannot remove mandatory rights. No party guarantees a sale or a particular price. This appointment does not compel the Seller to select a particular attorney or other ancillary service provider.

The parties may sign on paper or through an electronic route approved as lawful for this mandate. Every required person must review and sign the same identified version in their recorded capacity. Electronic signing of this mandate does not authorise electronic execution of a property sale agreement. The Seller and Agency must receive a complete copy and any signing evidence.
