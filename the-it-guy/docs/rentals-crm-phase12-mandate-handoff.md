# Rentals CRM Phase 12 — Landlord Mandate Handoff

Phase 12 connects a landlord lead at `Mandate pending` to the rental property-mandate foundation. Recording the handoff requires a selected managed property, signed date/time, signed-mandate evidence reference, and an explicit agent confirmation.

The handoff creates an active, confirmed rental property mandate and records its identifier and property relationship on the landlord CRM lead before advancing it to `Mandate signed`.

It does not generate a mandate, send it for signature, store the signed document itself, or treat the agent confirmation as independent legal proof.
