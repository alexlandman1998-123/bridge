# Attorney coordination Phase 9 — graduated expansion

Phase 9 turns a clean one-organisation pilot into an immutable, separately approved expansion plan. It validates planning and approval only; it never changes the feature flag or production cohort.

Every wave may at most double the active cohort. Each proposed organisation must prove transfer, bond and cancellation attorney readiness, support readiness and retained evidence. Between waves the release must preserve at least 24 hours of observation, four successful actions per role, propagation p95 no higher than 120 seconds, Televent parity, attribution integrity, zero safety incidents and separate authorization.

Targeting stays default-off using logged organisation-ID allowlists. The kill switch and owned rollback within 15 minutes remain mandatory. Run `npm run check:attorney-coordination-phase9`; only `APPROVED` permits a separately operated wave.
