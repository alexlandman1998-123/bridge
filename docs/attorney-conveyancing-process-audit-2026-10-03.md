# South African conveyancing workflow audit — 3 October 2026

The Attorney matter workspace has a credible structure for ordinary sale-linked transfers, new bonds and existing-bond cancellations. The simplest next step is to retain its stages and improve applicability, evidence and handoffs inside existing tasks. A larger task list would not resolve the most important issues found here.

This review inspected **89 catalogue tasks across 14 workspace stages: 49 transfer, 18 bond and 22 cancellation**. Some catalogue entries are historical or conditional; an ordinary matter should not receive all 89 tasks. The [task-by-task appendix](attorney-conveyancing-task-audit-appendix-2026-10-03.md) records every task, its current catalogue inputs, documents, confirmation items and proposed minimum review items.

The owning product is the primary Arch9 transaction workspace in `the-it-guy/`, with shared document rules and database safeguards in `supabase/`. This is an audit of local source and local checks. It does not certify a firm's legal compliance or establish what is currently deployed. No workflow changes, database changes or releases were made for this audit. Earlier task-save performance changes in this chat remain separate.

## What “the standard process” means

There is a stable conveyancing spine, but the governing requirements come from several sources: legislation and regulations, Deeds Office practice, the agreement, the particular lender's instructions, municipal or scheme requirements, and the firm's compliance procedures. A bank's published process is evidence of that bank's practice, rather than a universal legal checklist. For example, the lender-led instruction, guarantee and coordinated registration sequence appears in [FNB's buying process](https://www.fnb.co.za/home-loans/learnBuying.html).

The correct design is a common process assembled from independent facts, with a responsible attorney's decision where legal interpretation is needed. A company buying from a trust with mixed funding should combine those requirements; it should not create a separate entire workflow for that combination. Different instruments, such as a share-block interest, a release of security or certain endorsements, still need explicit classification. The existing specialist hold mechanism is useful for that boundary.

## Safeguards already present

The September review described earlier weaknesses. The current source now includes a versioned conditional plan, provisional handling for unknown finance or seller-bond facts, party-specific capacity decisions tied to current facts, specialist holds, cash/security handoffs, separate tax routes and per-seller withholding decisions. Do not rebuild those features because an older report lists them as missing.

The database migrations enforce objective restrictions at signing and lodgement milestones. They check current facts, applicable documents, critical task outcomes and cross-attorney readiness. Changes to important evidence withdraw readiness; the registration guard also rechecks current evidence. Ordinary work can proceed concurrently, and a documented task completion does not automatically certify lodgement. The workbench explicitly distinguishes its checklist guidance from legal readiness.

Keep that distinction: **work recorded → applicable evidence reviewed → authorised milestone attested**. A task percentage is not proof that a matter can be registered. Keep professional-only evidence, financial information and client progress separate.

## Findings requiring attention

The findings distinguish a directly observed mismatch from a legal/process interpretation that needs professional review. Priority reflects potential impact, not evidence that a live matter has failed.

### 1. Conditional cancellation signing is enforced as universal

**Confirmed rule mismatch; high priority.** `seller_cancellation_documents_signed` says “where required”, but the readiness guard includes it in the cancellation critical-task list and requires `completed`. Recording a truthful not-applicable outcome cannot satisfy that guard. The plan selects every cancellation task for an active cancellation lane.

The smallest correction is a recorded lender/instrument applicability decision inside this task. Require signed seller documents when instructed or legally required; otherwise require an attorney-reviewed reason and the actual bondholder authority. Do not remove consent or guarantee safeguards. Evidence: [task catalogue](/Users/alexanderlandman/the-it-guy/the-it-guy/src/constants/attorneyWorkflowStages.js:1145), [critical readiness list](/Users/alexanderlandman/the-it-guy/supabase/migrations/20260926132034_attorney_phase1_readiness_gates.sql:147), [lane task selection](/Users/alexanderlandman/the-it-guy/the-it-guy/src/services/attorneyWorkflow/matterWorkflowPlanService.js:102).

### 2. Non-resident withholding payment is required too early for some cases

**Confirmed pre-lodgement payment-proof requirement; timing interpretation requires review; high priority.** The financial model and SQL guard require each applicable seller's withholding `paymentReference` before transfer lodgement readiness. SARS distinguishes withholding and remittance: its guidance gives resident purchasers 14 days and non-resident purchasers 28 days after withholding to remit. Payment to SARS must not be confused with the earlier decision and arrangement to withhold. [SARS section 35A guide, Payment Process](https://www.sars.gov.za/guide-to-amounts-to-be-withheld-when-a-non-resident-sells-immovable-property-in-south-africa-sa/).

Have the conveyancer validate the relevant payment event and any directive conditions. Use the existing review task for applicability, calculation, reserved funds, owner and deadline before the relevant milestone; require payment evidence when remittance has occurred or is due. Carry unresolved remittance into final-account close-out. Do not weaken the separate SARS transfer-duty receipt gate. Evidence: [financial decision checks](/Users/alexanderlandman/the-it-guy/the-it-guy/src/services/attorneyWorkflow/transferPhase4Policy.js:121), [SQL payment-proof guard](/Users/alexanderlandman/the-it-guy/supabase/migrations/20260926142602_attorney_phase4_tax_clearance_conditions.sql:285).

### 3. Electrical proof is a blanket transaction blocker

**Confirmed source rule; applicability requires correction/review; high priority.** The canonical electrical document rule applies whenever context is `transaction`, with a lodgement blocker. The attorney resolver also adds it for resale/private-sale matters; neither inspected rule qualifies it by the presence of an electrical installation. Setting the broad compliance-applicability answer to “no” does not by itself remove that canonical rule. Electrical regulation 7 concerns electrical installations, certificate/test-report requirements and ownership changes; it does not establish that every vacant-land transaction has an installation. [Electrical Installation Regulations, regulations 7 and 9](https://www.labour.gov.za/DocumentCenter/Regulations%20and%20Notices/Regulations/Occupational%20Health%20and%20Safety/eir2009.pdf).

Use the existing property applicability review to establish installation, certificate scope, issue date and subsequent alterations. Make the canonical rule consistent with that reviewed decision. Preserve the applicable safety requirement. Cape Town water compliance already exists in shared document rules; the issue is visibility and consistent application rather than adding another task. [City of Cape Town submission process](https://www.capetown.gov.za/city-connect/apply/municipal-services/water-and-sanitation/submit-a-certificate-of-compliance-of-water-installation-on-transfer-of-ownership/). Evidence: [canonical electrical rule](/Users/alexanderlandman/the-it-guy/supabase/migrations/202605250001_canonical_document_system_phase1.sql:460), [attorney resolver](/Users/alexanderlandman/the-it-guy/the-it-guy/src/services/attorneyWorkflow/attorneyDocumentRequirementsResolver.js:621), [municipality-scoped water rule](/Users/alexanderlandman/the-it-guy/supabase/migrations/202607170005_comprehensive_seller_document_matrix_p0_2.sql:83).

### 4. Multiple registered bonds are not a first-class readiness set

**Observed modelling limitation; high priority for relevant matters.** The catalogue accepts scalar cancellation bank, account and figures-expiry fields and one lane; no repeatable bond/account readiness register was found in the audited workflow models. Multiple attachments or a note may preserve information, but do not prove that every registered bond has current figures, consent and a reconciled allocation.

Add repeatable security rows inside existing cancellation tasks: registered bond reference, affected property/share, lender, loan account(s), responsible attorney, disposition, figures/validity, consent, allocation and settlement. A registered bond and a loan account are not necessarily one-to-one. Block the relevant milestone on unresolved rows rather than duplicating the whole matter. Review cancellation versus release and statutory exceptions; the [Firstrand v Registrar judgment](https://www.saflii.org/za/cases/ZAGPPHC/2021/631.html) illustrates that consent/instrument questions can require a different route. Evidence: [bank/account fields](/Users/alexanderlandman/the-it-guy/the-it-guy/src/constants/attorneyWorkflowStages.js:994), [figures expiry field](/Users/alexanderlandman/the-it-guy/the-it-guy/src/constants/attorneyWorkflowStages.js:1073).

### 5. Plan order and cancellation action coverage disagree with the workspace

**Confirmed local verification failures; medium priority.** The plan generator takes catalogue order, placing title and existing-bond tasks after party-capacity work. Workspace phases place them in instruction/file opening. `matter-workflow-plan.test.mjs:256` fails comparing those sequences; the task membership is the same. Align canonical order and presentation rather than deleting the assertion.

The cancellation action verifier reports missing actions for `cancellation_guarantee_allocation_review`, `cancellation_consent_confirmed` and `cancellation_simultaneous_lodgement_confirmed`. This is an action-catalogue inconsistency, not proof that the generic workbench cannot save these tasks. Complete and verify the actual action path for all 22 current tasks. Evidence: [plan selection](/Users/alexanderlandman/the-it-guy/the-it-guy/src/services/attorneyWorkflow/matterWorkflowPlanService.js:91), [workspace phases](/Users/alexanderlandman/the-it-guy/the-it-guy/src/services/attorneyWorkflow/transferWorkspaceViewModel.js:40), [cancellation map](/Users/alexanderlandman/the-it-guy/the-it-guy/src/services/attorneyWorkflow/cancellationLaneJourneyMap.js).

### 6. Agreement conditions need a current decision, not only “checked”

**Checklist/control-depth improvement; high priority.** The OTP review already mentions suspensive conditions, but the audited task has broad confirmations rather than a repeatable condition register with deadline, responsible person, fulfilment/valid waiver/extension and evidence. No dedicated current-condition decision was found in the audited lodgement guard. A bank approval letter alone does not resolve every agreement condition.

Keep substantive review in `otp_source_docs_checked`; capture only conditions that actually apply and revalidate the decision after amendments or expiry. Cover deposit/guarantee dates, linked sale, approvals and occupation terms there, without adding a separate stage for each. Written agreement and authority requirements must be reviewed under the applicable [Alienation of Land Act](https://www.gov.za/documents/alienation-land-act-24-mar-2015-1035). Evidence: [current OTP task](/Users/alexanderlandman/the-it-guy/the-it-guy/src/constants/attorneyWorkflowStages.js:368).

### 7. Party capacity and FICA need targeted decision prompts

**Discoverability/evidence improvement; high priority.** The models already handle multiple parties, companies, CCs, trusts, marital regimes, beneficial ownership and specialists. The audited attorney checklist does not explicitly expose a firm's RMCP risk decision, politically exposed/prominent-person assessment, sanctions result or conditional enhanced review. This finding concerns the Attorney evidence path; it is not a conclusion that the firm has no procedures elsewhere. FIC's current [Guidance Note 7B, dated 3 August 2026](https://www.fic.gov.za/document/guidance-note-7b-implementation-of-various-aspects-of-the-fic-act/) replaces revised 7A and addresses risk-based due diligence. Avoid imposing enhanced evidence on every low-risk client.

Within the existing per-party reviews, prompt for minority/guardianship or impaired capacity, applicable customary/foreign-law marital questions, business rescue/liquidation, and whether a company disposal needs additional approval under sections 112/115. A standard board resolution should not silently settle those questions. [Companies Act](https://www.justice.gov.za/legislation/acts/2008-071.pdf), [Matrimonial Property Act section 15](https://www.justice.gov.za/legislation/acts/1984-088.pdf). For trusts, review the deed, currently authorised trustees, decision/quorum and signatories, using the [Master's authority guidance](https://www.justice.gov.za/master/trust.html). These are conditional prompts and legal decisions, not universal new document demands. Evidence: [party requirements](/Users/alexanderlandman/the-it-guy/the-it-guy/src/services/matterScenarioProfile.js), [scoped party evidence](/Users/alexanderlandman/the-it-guy/the-it-guy/src/core/transactions/stageTwoPartyEvidence.js).

### 8. Some checklist answers imply an outcome the milestone forbids

**Confirmed presentation mismatch; medium priority.** The default transfer financial, document and milestone confirmation rows offer yes/no/not applicable, including lodged/prep/registered confirmations. Critical lifecycle outcomes are separately restricted by operational contracts and SQL. The mismatch can confuse the user without bypassing those guards.

Show not-applicable only where the underlying requirement can legally be inapplicable. Keep guidance, missing evidence and attorney attestations visibly distinct. Use the existing financial/security/lodgement panels to show the substantive decision instead of adding several duplicate “reviewed” ticks. Evidence: [confirmation rows](/Users/alexanderlandman/the-it-guy/the-it-guy/src/core/transactions/legalTaskWorkbenchModel.js:536), [operational contract](/Users/alexanderlandman/the-it-guy/the-it-guy/src/core/transactions/attorneyTaskOperationalContract.js).

### 9. Closure requires a particular product channel

**Confirmed product gate; medium priority.** Closure requires a post-registration, client-visible Arch9 journey update with the registration metadata and a buyer or seller audience. An appropriate communication recorded outside that channel does not satisfy the inspected model/SQL guard. The recipient test requires at least one applicable audience; it does not itself prove that all people entitled to an update were informed.

Retain communication evidence, but let the attorney record the agreed audience, channel, date and reference, including an externally sent communication. Keep substantive financial documents private and verify all appropriate recipients. Final accounts should also show settlement, payee verification, refunds and outstanding delivery obligations; the [LPC bookkeeping guidance](https://lpc.org.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/i-LPC-Guide-for-Attorneys-Bookkeeping-ito-Regulation-610i.pdf) discusses the particular exposure of conveyancing trust payments to interception fraud. Evidence: [closure model](/Users/alexanderlandman/the-it-guy/the-it-guy/src/services/attorneyWorkflow/stageSixClosureReview.js:14), [closure audience guard](/Users/alexanderlandman/the-it-guy/supabase/migrations/20260926191847_attorney_stage6_closure_audience_gates.sql).

### 10. Clearance validity needs one reviewed interpretation across layers

**Confirmed conservative date rule; interpretation review; medium priority.** Frontend and SQL require recorded clearance expiry after today's date, excluding the recorded expiry date itself. Municipal issue date is optional; the 60-day interval is checked only when it is present. Body-corporate and HOA reviews also require future dates, although their applicable issuer terms must be distinguished from the statutory municipal period.

The municipal certificate has a 60-day statutory validity period under section 118(1A), with statutory exceptions elsewhere in section 118. [Municipal Systems Act hosted by DPSA, section 118](https://www.dpsa.gov.za/ethicsofficerguide/resources/lg/Municipal%20Systems%20Act_up%20to%20July2024.pdf). Resolve inclusive/exclusive end-date handling with a conveyancer and relevant registry, record issue date for new certificates and use issuer-specific conditions for scheme/HOA evidence. Do not invent uniform 60-day validity for them. For sectional title, section 15B allows amounts paid or provision satisfactory to the body corporate; account for the applicable certification route and exceptions. [Sectional Titles Act hosted by the Chief Surveyor-General](https://csg.dlrrd.gov.za/ACT95OF1986.pdf). Evidence: [date policy](/Users/alexanderlandman/the-it-guy/the-it-guy/src/services/attorneyWorkflow/transferPhase4Policy.js:5).

## Simplest corrective package

Retain the six transfer stages and four stages in each supporting lane. First correct the order/action inconsistencies and align the high-impact applicability and timing rules in findings 1–3. These are smaller changes than a new workflow design. Do not fix only the frontend when a shared document rule or SQL gate owns the restriction.

Next improve existing reviews: a small agreement-condition list, repeated security/account rows, conditional capacity/RMCP prompts, lender-specific applicability, consistent expiry treatment and channel-neutral closure evidence. Surface the authoritative decisions already available in the financial, party, security and lodgement panels. Avoid requiring the same proof to be uploaded again for another attorney; each responsible attorney should retain their own reviewed handoff/attestation.

Keep rare instrument questions in the existing specialist route with an owner, reason, applicable evidence and decision. Review inheritance/endorsement, standalone finance, agricultural/title and share-block scenarios before describing them as fully supported. Current sources and registry practice must be checked for each specialist implementation; this audit did not establish all relevant commencement notices or local registry circulars. The [2024 Deeds Registries amendment record](https://www.gov.za/documents/acts/deeds-registries-amendment-act-20-2024-english-afrikaans-10-dec-2024) is one reason historical Deeds Act PDFs alone should not be treated as a complete current practice manual.

Remove repeated substantive OTP review from instruction receipt, keep old task keys for history, and label internal due-date targets as internal. The operational contract's 1/2/3/5 business-day targets are not statutory conveyancing deadlines. No new numbered delivery programme or additional general-purpose stages are needed.

**The 95% target remains unproven.** Task counts and permutations cannot establish the percentage of actual conveyancing matters covered. Define the denominator first: ordinary sale-linked transfers in the target firms, or all conveyancing instructions including standalone and specialist instruments. Review a representative anonymised cohort, for example 100–200 recent matters across those firms, and record the actual party/funding/property/lender combinations.

Count a matter as covered only when it can record the correct instrument, all required parties/security items and applicable evidence, complete the real handoffs and reach the correct end state without invented approvals, false N/A answers or off-system legal decisions hidden from the record. Report observed coverage and the exceptions separately; a specialist hold demonstrates safe detection, not successful full-process coverage. Frequency matters more than enumerating every theoretically possible combination.

Have practising transfer, bond and cancellation conveyancers review the task appendix, the timing/applicability findings and representative files against their current lender manuals and Deeds Office practices. That is the remaining professional validation needed before claiming broad readiness. Browser walkthroughs, live rules/deployment identity, real documents, firm RMCPs and private lender manuals were not verified in this audit.
