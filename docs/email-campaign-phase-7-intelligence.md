# Arch9 Email Campaigns — Phase 7: Performance intelligence

Phase 7 provides an executive view across campaigns instead of forcing teams to interpret isolated campaign reports.

## Included

- Tenant-isolated daily performance view for sent, delivered, opened, clicked, bounced and unsubscribed recipient records.
- Subscription-category performance view to compare the topics recipients engage with.
- A compact seven-day delivery-volume trend, current category signal, delivery-rate headline, and bounce/unsubscribe guardrail message on the campaign overview.

## Interpretation

Delivery, bounce and unsubscribe are operational signals. Opens remain indicative because privacy protection can trigger or block tracking pixels. Click rate is shown against delivered records and is the stronger engagement signal for link-driven campaigns.

This phase uses security-invoker views and does not add a public reporting endpoint.
