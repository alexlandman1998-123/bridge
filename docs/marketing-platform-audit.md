# Arch9 marketing platform: repository audit and implementation roadmap

Audit date: 22 October 2026. Base commit: `44eaf1164`, plus the current local working tree.

## Decision

**Evolve Arch9 in place. Keep its property and transaction systems, extend its existing client identity and audience systems, and continue using providers for delivery.** The inspected architecture does not justify another frontend framework, database, CRM backbone or general automation service at this stage.

Arch9 already has substantially more marketing functionality than the brief assumes. Permanent client identities, reviewed duplicate merges, client timelines, attribution, dynamic audiences, an email content studio, email delivery jobs, WhatsApp campaigns, WhatsApp conversations and Facebook listing publication all have implementation evidence. Rebuilding these would duplicate working assets.

The immediate gaps are the connections between those assets and their operational guarantees. In particular, CRM identity and channel contact stores are not yet one dependable marketing model; email dispatch needs recovery and category-consent hardening; and the existing shared email inbox is organisation-specific. General workflows, full property-aware audience rules and usage charging remain additional product work.

Recommendation: sell a connected property-agency workflow first. Do not promise broad GoHighLevel parity or unlimited communications before those operational and commercial boundaries are verified.

## Priority technical findings

### F1 — High: dynamic audiences are only as fresh as a disconnected projection

`email_campaign_refresh_contact_projection` in the [email foundation](../supabase/migrations/20260909113000_email_campaigns_foundation.sql) projects operational contacts and the latest related lead into email contacts, then records source lineage. It is service-role-only. Searches of application services, server/API handlers and Edge Functions found its definition and contract assertions, but no runtime caller. This does not rule out an external/manual job; none was verified.

The existing [client identity migration](../supabase/migrations/20261009113042_agency_client_identity_and_reviewed_merge.sql) deliberately preserves original source records when updating/merging identities. The email tag service can also create an email contact directly. Consequently, editing a canonical profile or a lead is not established to refresh every channel projection. The current refresh additionally selects a single latest enquiry and does not model all buyer preferences. Repeated source emails in one refresh deserve an executable regression case because the upsert targets one row per organisation/email.

**Impact:** a smart list can evaluate its stored rules correctly while using stale or incomplete agency data. Contacts can still be recreated independently for WhatsApp, contrary to the target outcome.

**Action:** keep `agency_client_profiles` as the durable agency-scoped identity; add explicit channel/source resolution and a bounded, idempotent projection update path. Preserve historical aliases and channel-purpose consent. Test profile edits, changed emails, merges, new enquiries, shared phones and multi-role contacts. Record projection time/lag. Do not promise automatic freshness until this path runs and is observed.

### F2 — High: category opt-out after scheduling is not rechecked at email dispatch

The [dispatch preparation SQL](../supabase/migrations/20260918143000_dynamic_audience_campaign_delivery.sql) checks category subscription while snapshotting recipients. The [preference centre](../supabase/functions/email-preference-centre/index.ts) can unsubscribe from one category while keeping global marketing consent opted in for another. The [worker](../supabase/functions/email-campaign-worker/handler.ts) rechecks suppression and global consent, but does not select the campaign subscription type or recheck `contact_email_subscriptions` immediately before sending.

**Impact:** a contact who declines listing alerts after a campaign is scheduled, but keeps agency news, can remain eligible to receive the queued listing alert. This is a source-traced scenario, not an observed live send or a legal compliance determination.

**Action:** add a fail-closed dispatch check for the campaign's active category and that recipient's current subscription. Add a regression test for category-only withdrawal, missing records, query errors and inactive categories. Reuse the existing global-consent and suppression checks.

### F3 — High: email campaign claims do not visibly recover interrupted execution

The [worker](../supabase/functions/email-campaign-worker/handler.ts) selects only queued jobs, changes them to `running`, claims queued recipients as `sending`, and releases the job at the end. Provider fetch errors are not caught per recipient. `locked_at` is stored, but no stale-job reclamation path was found in the inspected scheduler/worker or other Edge handlers. A process exit can strand the job; a recipient left `sending` also lacks a visible reconciliation path.

**Impact:** bulk mail can stop indefinitely despite having a job table and provider idempotency keys. Setting `attempts: 1` on claim also fails to retain an increasing job-attempt count. Daily budget accounting across concurrently claimed campaigns needs a separate concurrency test; reading a count is not an atomic reservation.

**Action:** adapt the existing [durable lead email outbox](../supabase/migrations/20261008173528_durable_lead_agent_email_outbox.sql), which already demonstrates bounded leases and claim tokens. Add bounded attempts, retry/backoff, stale-claim reconciliation, per-organisation capacity control and visible failed/uncertain states. Reuse stable provider idempotency keys; reconcile uncertain provider results before resending.

### F4 — High: webhook deduplication can prevent repairing partial campaign processing

The [Resend webhook](../supabase/functions/resend-webhook/index.ts) inserts a shared webhook audit event before campaign event/recipient processing. If downstream processing fails after the audit insert, a retried event encounters the unique audit entry and returns success as a duplicate. The existing calendar-receipt path explicitly processes before that shared dedupe, acknowledging the repair problem for calendar delivery.

**Impact:** a failed campaign update or suppression write may not be repaired by provider retries. Some recipient/suppression writes also do not inspect their returned errors. Signature verification and deduplication are valuable, but do not make this path transactionally complete.

**Action:** make campaign event application transactional/idempotent or resume audit events in failed/incomplete states. Add failure-injection tests between audit, campaign event, recipient update and suppression writes, plus callback-before-send-record and out-of-order terminal-state cases. Do not alter operational email receipt behaviour without its focused checks.

### F5 — High before wider agency rollout: permission policy differs between CRM and channel contacts

Client identity visibility uses [source visibility and agency scope](../supabase/migrations/20261009113042_agency_client_identity_and_reviewed_merge.sql); client profiles can be hidden if a linked source is outside an agent's scope. The email foundation's `email_marketing_contacts_member` policy uses organisation membership, and WhatsApp's [read policies](../supabase/migrations/20260913134659_whatsapp_campaigns.sql) allow active organisation members. Thus agency-wide channel contact visibility is broader than assigned-client visibility by design in those definitions.

**Impact:** an agency expecting agents to see only their own contacts may get different behaviour in marketing screens. This is an identified policy mismatch requiring effective-schema verification, not a demonstrated cross-tenant breach. Latest hosted grants/RLS were not inspected.

**Action:** choose principal-wide marketing access and assigned-agent drafting rules explicitly. Test two agencies, two agents in one agency, branch managers, principals, suspended users and merged clients through the actual endpoint/RLS boundary. Keep broad access only where the product intends it.

### F6 — Medium now, high at volume: client-side aggregation and unbounded reads

The [marketing overview](../the-it-guy/src/services/marketingOverviewService.js) fetches at most 2,000 leads and 100 email campaigns, and counts digital-card events from bounded 5,000-row fetches. These are truncation risks for totals, not proven database slow queries. The [email workspace](../the-it-guy/src/services/emailCampaignService.js) loads several full collections; campaign analytics fetches recipients without pagination. [Client identity loading](../the-it-guy/src/services/clientIdentityService.js) paginates but ultimately loads all profiles, identifiers and links into the browser.

**Action:** move dashboard counts to scoped server aggregates, paginate recipient/client lists and make truncated results explicit until replaced. Obtain realistic query plans and tenant-volume measurements before adding indexes. General custom-field JSON querying should not precede a known access pattern.

### F7 — Medium: operational readiness and commercial readiness are separate

The Revo inbox has a hard-coded organisation boundary and limited initial message retrieval. Email's billing adapter intentionally charges nothing. Several historical phase documents explicitly describe source-only foundations, while later handlers provide additional implementation. Existing [credential helpers](../supabase/functions/_shared/apiCredentials.ts) now reject legacy aliases unless explicitly allowed, but one email test suite still supplies old-style fake aliases.

**Action:** repair fixtures, verify supported runtime, maintain an integration readiness checklist with code/test/deployed/provider-approved/observed-delivery evidence, and define paid allowances before onboarding volume. Do not report either “just scaffolded” or “production ready” from document titles alone.

### Other debt: preserve useful differences, defer cosmetic unification

- `contacts`, identities, leads, rental/commercial contacts, marketing contacts and inbox senders overlap. Some are legitimate domain records/projections; the problem is absent explicit resolution/freshness, not simply the number of tables.
- Buyer/seller/developer/rental/attorney/bond workflows should not share one editable stage enum. Their legal and transaction gates differ. Reuse common task/event/delivery contracts while keeping domain rules.
- Compatibility branches in the CRM repository and assignment service handle historical schema drift. Remove one only after effective schema and callers are verified; a wholesale cleanup would increase release risk.
- Rate limiting exists in public intake, some signing/push operations and provider write paths. Uniform fairness, retries and provider quota handling across marketing were not established. Extend the existing controls rather than claiming rate limiting is wholly absent.
- Existing telemetry and error tables are useful. Queue lag, projection lag, dispatch attempt outcomes and failed webhook replay need specific alerts and an owner; adding another monitoring vendor is not a substitute.
- Reviewed browser credentials are guarded; Meta's newer integration path encrypts tokens, while compatibility reads of legacy token columns remain. No repository-wide secret scan or dependency/CVE audit was performed, so this report makes no “no secrets” or “no deprecated libraries” claim.

### Smallest useful pilot and definition of done

Pilot one consenting agency through one existing enquiry channel and its current CRM journey. Reuse capture and assignment rather than adding a new contact form or pipeline.

1. A new property enquiry is stored once with its source reference and resolves to the correct agency-scoped client identity; duplicate provider delivery does not create duplicate work.
2. The assigned agent sees the lead, property and next task. A principal sees unresolved assignments and overdue follow-up through existing task/reminder facilities.
3. The client profile shows that enquiry and activity, preserving any existing transactions and portal access. A reviewed duplicate merge retains the history.
4. Appropriate email/WhatsApp opt-in is recorded separately. Unknown permission creates no marketing eligibility; opting out after scheduling prevents delivery.
5. A saved audience reflects a changed contact/enquiry through the observed projection path. A multi-role client and two simultaneous enquiries do not lose context.
6. Another agency cannot access the record, and agents/branches see only the agreed scope. Workflow replay and interrupted jobs neither duplicate tasks nor blindly resend messages.
7. Local tests cover those cases; any approved live acceptance separately records sender identity, provider response, persisted receipt and recipient outcome.

Deliberately outside this pilot: arbitrary custom fields/pipeline designers, a general visual workflow editor, new social networks, telephony, AI publishing, automatic charging and international transaction changes. This is a proposal for product scope; no broad feature implementation was started without agreeing that scope.
